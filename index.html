ethdebug post: figures and demos

The interactive figures for the ethdebug post on the Argot blog, and the demos behind them. The post introduces ethdebug/format.

Demos

  • Step through a transaction: soldb, Walnut's debugger, for Solidity and Fe.
  • Storage inspector: each variable's bytes, found through solc's ethdebug pointers.
  • Ten-line stepper: a source stepper and its output for Solidity and Fe.
